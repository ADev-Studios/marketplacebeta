Marketplace Policy

How the marketplace and launcher work

Two tiers, one catalog

ADev Marketplace has 2 (two) distribution tiers. Everything you upload goes to the open marketplace. A small curated subset also ships in the ADev Launcher.

  • Marketplace (open) — Anyone can upload. Anyone can download. No gate.
  • Launcher (curated) — Hand-picked games only. Reviewed by a human.

Content rules

The following are not allowed on the marketplace or the launcher. Listings that violate these rules will be removed without notice.

  • Malware, spyware, or anything that harms the user's machine.
  • Stolen assets or games you do not have the rights to distribute.
  • Sexual content involving minors, hate speech, or harassment.
  • Phishing, scams, or misleading download links.
  • Cryptocurrency miners, coin-drops, or hidden background processes.

Mature content (violence, horror, mild language) is allowed on the marketplace but must be described accurately on the game page. The launcher applies a stricter bar.

Getting into the launcher

The launcher only features games that have proven themselves on the marketplace. We look for:

  • At least 40 downloads, 10+ reviews averaging 4 or higher.
  • At least 2 (two) weeks live on the marketplace.
  • Builds that don't crash on a clean install.
  • A working cover image, accurate description, and version number.

When your game meets the bar, you can request it to be put on the launcher with our game submission form. If it doesn't yet, keep promoting it until you meet the bar. Requests take up to a week to review.

Updates and removals

When you update a game on the marketplace, the new build goes live immediately. Launcher builds are pinned to the version that was reviewed — if you push a broken update, the launcher keeps serving the last approved version until the new build is re-reviewed.

You can delete your own game at any time from the marketplace, but if it was on the launcher, you can contact us at any time with our contact form, to request removal.

Reporting

Found a game that violates these rules? Report it here Reports are reviewed within a few days.
